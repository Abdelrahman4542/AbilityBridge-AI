# AbilityBridge-AI

AI-Powered Communication & Accessibility Platform
💡 Project Idea

AbilityBridge AI is an Arabic-first AI-powered accessibility platform designed to help three main groups communicate and interact with their surroundings more independently:

🧏 Deaf or hard-of-hearing individuals

🗣️ People with speech difficulties or who cannot speak

👁️ Blind or visually impaired individuals

The platform provides three main AI assistants: SignBridge, which enables bidirectional communication between deaf and hearing people by translating sign language into text/speech and spoken language into sign representation; AAC-AI, which helps people with speech difficulties express themselves by predicting personalized phrases from text, icons, gestures, and conversational context and converting them into natural speech; and VisionAssist, which uses computer vision, object detection, OCR, and scene understanding to describe the user's surroundings through speech. The platform integrates Computer Vision, Natural Language Processing, Speech Processing, Deep Learning, and Personalization into one adaptive communication system. Arabic will be the primary language and the core scope of the project, while English support will be considered as an optional extension if development time allows.
